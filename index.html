The Concept

Drinks straight from the tropics

Roots is a tropical bar in Batroun built around one simple idea — your cocktail belongs inside the fruit. We hollow out fresh pineapples, coconuts and melons and fill them with handcrafted cocktails. It looks unreal, it tastes better, and it's the kind of drink people photograph before the first sip.
